The CSSS Executive Team consists of multiple leading executive positions and act as the official voice of the computing science body.
They represent our interests to the School of Computing Science, the Faculty of Applied Science, and Simon Fraser Student Society.

Executives are elected to represent and serve the computing science student body, and have a responsibility to create an inclusive community, organize events, and provide resources to our members.
Learn more from our [Constitution](https://github.com/CSSS/public-docs/blob/master/constitutions/Constitution_November_2025.pdf).
