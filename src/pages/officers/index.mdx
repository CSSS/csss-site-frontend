The CSSS Executive Team consists of twelve leading executive positions.
They is elected every year to represent and serve the computing science student body.
Executives have a responsibility to create an inclusive community, organize events, and provide resources to the students they represent.

The team represents the computing science student body to the School of Computing Science, the Faculty of Applied Science, and Simon Fraser Student Society (SFSS)
